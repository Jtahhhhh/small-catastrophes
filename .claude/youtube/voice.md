# voice.md — Small Catastrophes

Read by every /yt-* skill. Copy to `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` at the start of a session.

## Who I am talking to

One curious adult in the US, UK, Canada or Australia, watching on a phone or TV in the evening,
who has never thought about how the boring systems around them (power, water, trucks, the internet)
actually keep running, and would like to know what happens the hour they stop.

## How I actually talk

Faceless channel. Narrated by a calm British male voice (Kokoro bm_george) over hand-drawn gothic
illustrations. Short declarative sentences. Present tense. Hour-by-hour structure: "At eight
o'clock on a Monday morning, the internet stops." / "Not slow. Not patchy. Gone, everywhere, for
twenty-four hours." Dry, quiet understatement rather than hype. Each episode ends on a small,
human, slightly unsettling observation, not a call to action.

## Words I never use

"unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "smash that like button", "insane",
"mind-blowing", "you won't believe", "literally", emojis in narration.

## Words I do use

Exact clock times ("By ten past nine…"), plain numbers spelled for the ear, "quietly", "nobody
notices at first", "and then".

## What I will not claim

Any number without a source listed in the script's `sources`. Real outages are named with year and
place (Rogers Canada 2022, Tonga 2022, UK fuel 2021, Ambassador Bridge 2022). Speculative parts are
framed as "what if", never as prediction. No medical, financial or political advice. The video is
AI-assisted and is disclosed as such on YouTube.

## My format

Long video 6–8 minutes, no intro card, opens on the first clock time within 3 seconds. Chapters in
the description. Three Shorts per episode cut from shots of the long video, under 60 s, each with
its own hook line. English only. Facebook gets only the Shorts as Reels.
